Our Story

15 Years of Shaping Young Minds

Rising Star Public School was founded in 2009 with a singular mission: to create an educational institution that goes beyond rote learning and empowers students to think, create, and lead.

What started as a small school with a big dream has grown into a thriving community of over 1,500 students, 85 faculty members, and countless proud alumni who are making their mark across India and the world.

We are proud to be CBSE affiliated and committed to the holistic development of every child — academically, physically, emotionally, and socially.

Our Vision

A World Where Every Child Shines

Our vision is to be a centre of academic excellence that fosters intellectual curiosity, moral integrity, and a love for lifelong learning in every student who walks through our doors.

We envision a school where diversity is celebrated, potential is unlimited, and every child leaves not just with certificates but with the confidence, skills, and character to succeed in life.

"To ignite the spark of curiosity in every child and fan it into the flame of lifelong achievement."

Our Mission

What We Stand For Every Day

📚

Academic Excellence

To deliver a curriculum that challenges students to think critically, explore deeply, and achieve their personal best in every subject and discipline.

🌱

Character Development

To instil values of honesty, empathy, responsibility, and resilience — building young people of strong character who contribute positively to society.

🤝

Inclusive Community

To create a safe, welcoming environment where every child — regardless of background — feels seen, valued, and empowered to reach their potential.

🚀

Future Readiness

To equip students with 21st-century skills — digital literacy, communication, collaboration, and innovation — preparing them for careers and lives that don't yet exist.
